# Competitive analysis

**Status:** Preliminary feature-level research, checked November 3, 2026. This is not a market-size study or legal clearance. Do not claim Resqiva is the first or that competitors lack features without evidence.

## Adjacent products

| Product | Publicly described capability | Relationship to Resqiva |
|---|---|---|
| [Too Good To Go](https://www.toogoodtogo.com/en-us/how-does-the-app-work) | Customers discover discounted surplus food, reserve and pay, then collect in a scheduled store window. Its Surprise Bags are a prominent format. | Directly overlaps surplus-food discovery and pickup. Resqiva's planned distinction is to compose item-level inventory into constrained meals before checkout. This is a product hypothesis, not a verified exclusive capability. |
| [Flashfood](https://help.flashfood.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049204914-What-is-Flashfood) | Customers browse discounted grocery products, purchase in the app, and collect from a participating store. | Directly overlaps discounted grocery inventory and pickup. Resqiva aims to add inventory-grounded multi-item meal planning. |
| [Mealime](https://www.mealime.com/) | Personalized meal plans, dietary preferences, recipes, grocery lists, and cooking guidance. | [SuperCook](https://www.supercook.com/) | Recipe discovery based on ingredients users have. | Adjacent ingredient-to-recipe matching; not a grocery surplus checkout comparison. |

Related open-source projects include [FoodRescurer-AI](https://github.com/yashasl/FoodRescurer-AI), [Foodlink](https://github.com/p7yush/Foodlink), [Second Serve](https://github.com/roanshdesai/second-serve), and [ResQMeal](https://github.com/anupamchaubey/ResQMeal). Their public descriptions cover overlapping food rescue, donation coordination, delivery, or meal-planning ideas. Review their current code and licenses before making detailed claims about feature differences.

## Intended positioning

Resqiva is intended to combine a consumer grocery-surplus catalog, quantity- and pickup-aware meal bundles, deterministic constraint checks, and PayPal checkout. The optimizer must ground each chosen item, quantity, and price in current inventory. This combination is a proposed differentiator; the research above does not establish that no other product offers it.
