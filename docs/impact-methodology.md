# Impact methodology

**Status:** Measurement plan. No impact totals have been measured.

## What the prototype can count

From verified database records, report:

- quantity of surplus units included in a paid Sandbox order;
- number of paid demo orders and number of fulfilled pickups;
- total customer price and arithmetic savings compared with recorded original prices;
- number of bundles rejected for stock, budget, pickup, or dietary constraints.

Only count a unit as purchased after PayPal capture is verified. Only count a pickup as completed after a merchant confirmation is recorded. Keep demo-data counts separate from real merchant/customer activity.

## What these metrics do not mean

Purchased or fulfilled surplus units are not direct proof of avoided landfill, reduced emissions, improved nutrition, or long-term customer savings. Do not convert them to kilograms of waste, CO₂e, or health outcomes without a defensible external methodology and appropriate caveats. Do not extrapolate seeded simulations to a city, retailer, or population.

Any optimizer benchmark is a software metric, not food-waste impact. Label simulated inventory and results explicitly as simulated.
